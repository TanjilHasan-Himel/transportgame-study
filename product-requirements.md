# Product requirements and PM priorities

**DOCUMENTED, E01/E02:** Bengal Transport Tycoon explores Bangladesh bus-company management and separate operating scenarios. Intended players/learners are proposed personas, not validated customer segments. The owner reports approximately one month of development with daily work ongoing as of 6 November 2026; exact start date and hours are UNKNOWN.

## Product hypothesis and MVP

**INFERRED reconstructed hypothesis:** a connected preparation → dispatch → journey → result loop can make management decisions understandable; an isolated scenario workspace can support exploration. Neither learning value nor enjoyment is validated by a formal study.

**PROPOSED MVP:** one guest company, one repeatable bus trip, visible blockers/actions, explicit route precision, reconciled financial result and safe reload. Scenarios must not change Career-owned state. Complete multiplayer, live navigation, professional forecasts and multiple operating divisions are outside verified scope.

## Prioritization

| Priority | Work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Preparation, dispatch, settlement and reload | AC1–AC3: reproducible full journey with cash and replay assertions |
| P0 | Trustworthy language and assumptions | AC4/AC5: distinguish simulated/approximate/scenario outputs from real facts |
| P1 | Cloud correctness and security | AC6: contract agreement and two-user/device checks |
| P1 | Accessibility, responsiveness, performance and user validation | Measured representative-device and task results, not screenshot taste alone |
| Later | Progression, advanced fleet/body systems and other businesses | Separate small specs only after the core loop proves usable |

This ordering is PROPOSED analyst prioritization, not a recorded historical roadmap decision or an implementation commitment. ## Outcomes and contribution

**VERIFIED bounded outputs:** software artifacts, test assertions and sample screenshots. **UNKNOWN:** retention, adoption, conversion, commercial revenue and efficiency improvement. Proposed future measures include unassisted task completion, blocker-resolution time, unexpected cash deltas and reload failures; no baseline or improvement percentage exists.

The owner's role is concept/domain requirements, scope and feedback. Development and reporting were AI-assisted; this is not presented as independently hand-coded work. No hypothetical operator interview is presented as research already performed.
